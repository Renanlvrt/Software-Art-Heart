The outer coil has a natural 2mm separation from the outer skin surface to account for clothing tolerances. Inner coil has zero tolerance.

Litz wires have 48 strands of unwound copper separated by an air gap from each other to simulate the individual insulation. They are all soldered at the end.

Hairs are around 1.5cm in length.

Hair density is 4.55/cm^2 and covers a patch of 75 * 75mm (256 hairs). Only small patch used to avoid simulation crash.

An additional simulation will be repeated on "tet hair.step" to simulate MHz frequencies.

All simulations are performed on skin of thickness 20mm to match research data. "tet skinny.step" simulates the inaccurate 10mm thickness which, in reality, is too thin for the chest/abdominal region even for patients of BMI as low as 17.
